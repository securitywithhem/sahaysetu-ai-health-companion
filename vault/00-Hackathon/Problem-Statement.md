# The Rural Healthcare Challenge in India

- **Symptom Ambiguity** — patients misjudge severity → anxiety or false reassurance
- **Unnecessary Visits** — long travel for minor ailments overloads facilities
- **Delayed Care** — real emergencies ignored until too late
- **Adherence Struggles** — chronic patients (diabetes, hypertension) miss doses
- **Guidance Gap** — no personalized preventive advice
- **Connectivity Barriers** — limited internet in remote areas
- **Fragmented Information** — doctors get incomplete patient history

> Millions lack timely healthcare guidance before reaching a medical professional.

## Current journey (broken)
Patient feels unwell → Googles it → gets confused → ignores symptoms *or* rushes to hospital → poor decision.

## Our journey (AI Health Companion)
Patient enters symptoms → AI analyzes safely → emergency red flags detected → clear triage recommendation → daily health tracking → better healthcare decisions.

## Stakeholder impact
| Stakeholder | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Patients | Better decisions, daily self-management guidance, improved adherence |
| Doctors | Cleaner reports, less admin work, better patient history insight |
| Health system | Fewer unnecessary visits, better preventive outcomes, rural access |
