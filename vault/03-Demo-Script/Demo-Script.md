## 3-minute pitch (spoken)
1. Hook (10s): "Millions of people in rural India decide between ignoring a
   symptom and traveling hours to a hospital — with no guidance in between."
2. Problem (30s): symptom ambiguity, delayed care, adherence struggles.
3. Solution (60s): rule engine decides, LLM explains — walk through the
   4 modules.
4. Why we're different (40s): offline-first, explainable, fail-safe design.
5. Impact (20s): patients / doctors / health system.
6. Close (20s): "Early guidance. Better decisions. Healthier lives."
